How to Request Account Deletion

To delete your Lekka account, send an email to hello@aquleo.in. There is no in-app delete button — email is the only deletion channel. Follow the steps below:

  1. Open your email app and compose a new email to hello@aquleo.in
  2. Set the subject line exactly to: Delete my Lekka account
  3. In the body, include your registered phone number (the number you used to sign in to Lekka) so we can identify your account
  4. Send the email — you will receive a confirmation reply from us within 48 hours
  5. Your account data will be permanently deleted within 30 days of your request
Example email To: hello@aquleo.in
Subject: Delete my Lekka account

Hi,
Please delete my Lekka account.
My registered phone number is: +91 XXXXXXXXXX
Thank you.
We will confirm your deletion request by reply within 48 hours and complete the deletion within 30 days.
02

What Gets Deleted

Lekka stores very little data on our servers. Here is exactly what gets deleted and when:

Data Where stored Deleted when?
Phone number (used for sign-in / OTP) Supabase authentication record Within 30 days of your email request
Ledger data — customers, transactions, notes On-device SQLite database (your phone only) Automatic when you uninstall the app
Your ledger data (customers, transactions) never leaves your device. Uninstalling the app permanently removes it — no request to us is needed for that.
03

What Is Retained After Deletion

Nothing. After your deletion request is processed, Aquleo Technologies retains no data about you whatsoever.

  • No phone number
  • No transaction history
  • No account records
  • No backups
We do not retain any data for legal, tax, or compliance purposes because Lekka does not process payments or hold financial records on our servers — all ledger data is yours, on your device only.
04

Before You Delete — Things to Know

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Once your account is deleted, you cannot recover your sign-in access. Your on-device ledger data will remain on your phone until you uninstall the app.
  • If you want to keep your data, export or note it down before requesting deletion
  • Deleting your account does not automatically uninstall the app from your device
  • If you reinstall Lekka in the future, you will start with a fresh account
  • Your on-device data is not affected by the account deletion request — uninstall the app to remove it from your phone
